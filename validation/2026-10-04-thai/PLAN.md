Windows RAM: sample `Win32_OperatingSystem.FreePhysicalMemory` and summed `Get-Process ollama*` working sets/private bytes nominally every 500ms plus collection overhead. Sample timestamp is at collection completion. Record actual intervals. Working sets are physical resident pages and summed shared pages can be counted twice; private bytes describe virtual commitment, not resident physical RAM. `/api/ps.size` is Ollama's allocation report, not an OS RAM measurement. Sampled peaks are lower bounds; brief spikes can be missed and short calls may have no samples. No claim of paging-free operation from free RAM alone.
